# material_asset_pair_training_r004

This technical training view joins real PrefabGraph material usage with the verified asset capability registry `asset_registry_r003`. Positive pairs come from source Material texture bindings. Negative pairs are semantic contrasts used only to teach ranking; they are not aesthetic negatives.

Material clusters are isolated across train/validation/test. Human labels, preference labels and aesthetic scores consumed: `0`. Every selected asset remains subject to Unity 2022 property, dependency, compile, readback and runtime gates.
